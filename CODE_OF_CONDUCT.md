# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Miskatonic Commons to be a welcoming, harassment-free place to
collaborate, for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's privacy; do not share others' personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and any other channel used for the project, and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers using GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting form for this repository (Security tab,
"Report a vulnerability") and mark the report as a conduct issue, or use
GitHub's built-in abuse reporting for content. Reports will be handled
confidentially and on a best-effort basis.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block
participants who do so.
